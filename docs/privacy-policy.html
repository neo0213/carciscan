Privacy Policy

CarciScan  ·  Last updated: April 2, 2026

CarciScan is an academic research application developed as part of a thesis project. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles your information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

CarciScan does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII). The application operates as follows:

  • Images and text — Ingredient labels or text you submit are sent to our analysis server solely to perform carcinogenicity classification. They are not stored on the server after the request is processed.
  • Scan history — Results are stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.
  • No account required — CarciScan does not require you to create an account or provide any personal details.

2. How We Use Your Information

The ingredient data you submit is used exclusively to:

  • Classify ingredients against the IARC carcinogenicity database.
  • Return a risk assessment result to your device.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the core functionality of the application.

3. Data Storage

All scan history is stored locally on your device using SQLite. You have full control over this data — you can rename, delete individual scans, or clear all history at any time directly within the app.

4. Third-Party Services

CarciScan communicates with our classification API hosted at carciscan.edwardgarcia.site to process ingredient data. No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are used.

5. Camera and Photo Library

The app requests access to your camera and photo library solely to capture or select product label images for analysis. Images are not stored by the server and are not shared with any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

CarciScan is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at neo0213@example.com.
